> Directed under `motionmaxxing` craft laws.  
> 0% AI slop: no corner badges, no deck titles, no fake web-component HUDs, cut on motion, zero dead frames.

---

## 1. PAGE Framework
* **Problem:** Remote desktop software is bloated, charges exorbitant subscriptions, disconnects after 5 minutes, and drains laptop batteries with inefficient video loops.
* **Audience:** Developers, engineering students, IT admins, and power users who need instant, unattended remote access to their workstation from anywhere.
* **Goal:** Make the viewer believe that remote access can be weightless, instant, and completely free without corporate strings attached.
* **Emotion:** Fluidity, liberation, raw speed, precision.

## 3. The One Idea: "The Zero-Mass Beam"
* **Thesis:** Remote computing should feel like extending your monitor cable through the air, not launching a corporate platform.
* **The Shape:** `Hook (The Heavy Wall) -> Turn (The Beam Cuts Through) -> Proof (Instant Remote Control & UAC pass) -> Handoff to Clean Mark`.

## 4. World Design & Hero Moment
* **Ground:** Dark brushed slate with micro-grid laser etchings (0.05 opacity).
* **Light:** Directional cyan edge lighting from the top-left; glowing emerald laser core that casts real specular reflections on the desktop glass.
* **Depth:** Dual z-plane: background desktop frame at z=-120px, active client viewport at z=0px, optical laser bridge hovering at z=+40px.
* **Sound:** Deep sub-bass surge (32Hz) during connection lock; crisp tactile mechanical click on the authentication pulse.
* **Hero Moment:** A 4K CAD/Code window streaming at full 60 FPS, panning across dual monitors in a single swipe, while a Windows UAC prompt is confirmed remotely without freeze or black screen.
